Problem With Aluminium

Understanding excessive aluminium usage and its effects on society.

Cooking Acidic Foods

Cooking acidic foods such as tomatoes, tamarind, vinegar-based dishes and citrus foods in aluminium cookware may increase the transfer of aluminium into food.

Frequent exposure may contribute to higher aluminium intake over time.

Daily Exposure

People are exposed to aluminium through:

  • Cookware
  • Foil packaging
  • Food containers
  • Beverage cans
  • Processed foods

Daily exposure often goes unnoticed.

Lack of Awareness

Many households are unaware of:

  • Safe cooking practices
  • Alternative cookware
  • Health concerns
  • Environmental impacts

Awareness is the first step toward safer and sustainable choices.

Key Awareness Areas

Important issues related to aluminium usage

Health Awareness

Safer Choices

Adopting alternatives can reduce exposure.

Why Reduce Aluminium Usage?

Reducing unnecessary aluminium exposure helps support healthier cooking practices, encourages awareness and promotes environmental sustainability.

Simple changes such as using stainless steel, glass containers and clay cookware can contribute to safer daily habits.
